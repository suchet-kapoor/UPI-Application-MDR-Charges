🌈 3. What changes, then?

Only this, pinky promise 🤙: big shops pay 0.4% above ₹2,000, max ₹300. Tiny vendors (≤₹1L/month) pay zero — even on ₹5,000 sale! Fuel, insurance, bijli-paani-gas, railways, mobile, school fees → flat ₹5 only. Share-market → 0.02% only.
